Privacy Policy for Boing Up

Effective Date: September 18, 2026
Last Updated: September 18, 2026

Boing Up is a mobile game developed and published by Entexel ("Entexel", "we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use Boing Up ("the Game").

Boing Up is intended for a mixed audience and may be used by both children and adults. Because children may use the Game, we apply additional protections to child users as described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Boing Up does not require players to provide their real name, home address, telephone number, or similar personal information in order to play.

However, depending on the features used, Boing Up and its service providers may process information such as:

  • A game or player identifier
  • Unity Authentication identifier
  • Google Play Games player identifier
  • Device and operating system information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from an IP address
  • App version
  • Game interactions
  • Game progress
  • High score
  • Achievement progress
  • Coin balance
  • Unlocked or purchased in-game items
  • Equipped cosmetic items
  • Game settings
  • Crash information
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Advertising interactions
  • Advertising identifiers for eligible adult users where permitted

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, health information, or precise GPS location.

2. Age Screening

Because Boing Up is intended for both children and adults, the Game may use a neutral age-screening process.

The age screen is designed to determine which privacy, advertising, account, and cloud-data rules apply to the player.

The age screen does not encourage players to provide a false age.

Users who are identified as children, and users whose age has not yet been established where required, are handled using additional privacy protections.

3. Children's Privacy

Protecting children's privacy is important to us.

For users identified as children, Boing Up does not intentionally use personal information for personalized or interest-based advertising.

We do not intentionally transmit prohibited persistent device identifiers from child users or users whose age is unknown where Google Play policies prohibit such processing.

Where applicable law requires verified parental consent before a child's personal information can be processed through a particular service, the relevant feature must not be activated for that child until the required consent has been obtained.

This may apply to cloud-saving and authentication features provided through Unity Gaming Services.

If the required parental consent has not been obtained, certain online or cloud-based features may be unavailable to the child.

Parents or legal guardians may contact us to:

  • Ask what information relating to their child is stored
  • Request correction of information
  • Request deletion of information
  • Withdraw previously provided consent where applicable
  • Ask questions about the Game's privacy practices

We may need information sufficient to identify the relevant player account before processing a request.

4. Unity Authentication and Cloud Saving

Boing Up uses services provided by Unity Technologies, which may include:

  • Unity Authentication
  • Unity Cloud Save
  • Unity Cloud Code
  • Other Unity Gaming Services necessary to operate game features

Unity Authentication creates a unique identifier that allows game information to be associated with the correct player.

Boing Up may use Unity Cloud Save and related services to store information such as:

  • High score
  • Coin balance
  • Achievement progress
  • Unlocked items
  • Purchased in-game items
  • Equipped items
  • Game progress
  • Other game-related settings or progress

These systems help provide features such as restoring progress after reinstalling the Game and synchronizing supported game information.

Unity may also process technical information required to provide these services.

For child users, Unity Authentication, Cloud Save, and related services that process child-user information are used only where the required parental consent has been obtained, when such consent is required by applicable law.

5. Google Play Games

On supported Android devices, Boing Up may use Google Play Games Services.

Google Play Games may provide features such as:

  • Player authentication
  • Player identification
  • Achievements
  • Game-related profile functionality
  • Recognition of returning players
  • Account-linked game functionality

Boing Up may receive a Google Play Games player identifier and game-related profile information made available through the service.

We do not receive a player's Google Account password.

Children using supervised Google Accounts may be subject to Google's parental controls and approval requirements.

Google processes information associated with Google Play Games according to Google's own privacy practices.

6. Advertising

Boing Up uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Advertisements may include formats such as:

  • Banner advertisements
  • Rewarded advertisements, where implemented

Google and its advertising technology may process information necessary to display, measure, secure, and manage advertisements.

Depending on the user's age, region, consent choices, and applicable law, this information may include:

  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Advertising interactions
  • Advertisement impressions
  • Advertisement clicks
  • Diagnostic information
  • Advertising identifiers for eligible users

7. Advertising for Children

Advertisements shown to users identified as children are treated differently from advertising shown to eligible older users.

For child users:

  • Personalized advertising is disabled
  • Interest-based advertising is disabled
  • Remarketing is not used
  • Advertising is configured for child-directed treatment
  • Advertising content is restricted to content appropriate for children
  • Advertising SDKs and configurations must comply with Google Play Families requirements
  • Prohibited persistent device identifiers are not intentionally transmitted

Users whose age has not been established are also treated conservatively where required by Google Play policy or applicable law.

8. Advertising for Older Users

Users who are identified as older users may receive advertising based on their privacy choices, location, applicable law, and device settings.

Where consent is legally required for personalized advertising, personalized advertising will only be enabled after the appropriate consent has been obtained.

Users may be able to change advertising or consent preferences through privacy controls provided in the Game, their Google Account, or their device settings.

9. Consent in Certain Regions

Privacy and advertising laws differ between countries and regions.

In regions where consent is required, players may be shown a privacy or consent message before certain advertising technologies are activated.

Players may be offered choices relating to:

  • Personalized advertising
  • Non-personalized advertising
  • Advertising storage
  • Advertising measurement
  • Other advertising-related data processing

Where parental consent is legally required for a child, consent must be obtained from a parent or legal guardian using an appropriate process before the relevant child-user data is processed.

10. How We Use Information

Information processed through Boing Up may be used to:

  • Operate the Game
  • Authenticate players
  • Save game progress
  • Restore game progress
  • Maintain scores
  • Maintain coin balances
  • Maintain achievement progress
  • Maintain ownership of in-game items
  • Synchronize supported game information
  • Provide advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Detect cheating
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Fix crashes and errors
  • Improve game stability
  • Maintain security
  • Comply with legal obligations

Children's information is not intentionally used for personalized advertising.

11. Local Data

Some information may be stored directly on the player's device.

This may include:

  • Music settings
  • Sound-effect settings
  • Vibration settings
  • Game preferences
  • Certain locally cached game information

Deleting Boing Up or clearing its application data may remove locally stored information.

Game information already stored through an approved cloud service may remain stored separately and may be restored if the player later uses the appropriate account or authentication method.

Boing Up uses third-party services necessary to operate certain features.

Google

Google services used by Boing Up may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Mobile Ads
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google Play Services

Unity Technologies

Unity services used by Boing Up may include:

  • Unity Engine
  • Unity Authentication
  • Unity Cloud Save
  • Unity Cloud Code
  • Unity Gaming Services

These companies process information according to their respective privacy policies, terms, and service requirements.

We encourage parents and adult players to review the privacy practices of these service providers.

13. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with service providers only where reasonably necessary to provide and support Boing Up.

This may include sharing or processing necessary for:

  • Authentication
  • Cloud storage
  • Advertising
  • Security
  • Fraud prevention
  • Diagnostics
  • Technical infrastructure
  • Legal compliance

Service providers may include companies such as Google and Unity Technologies.

We do not sell players' personal information in exchange for direct monetary payment.

Certain advertising-related practices involving adult users may be classified as "sharing", "targeted advertising", or similar terms under some privacy laws.

Such advertising practices are not used for users identified as children.

14. Data Retention

We retain game-related information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Game, maintain player progress, comply with legal requirements, maintain security, and resolve disputes.

Cloud-saved game information may remain associated with the player's authentication identifier until:

  • The player or parent requests deletion
  • We delete the information
  • The associated account is deleted
  • The service provider deletes the information according to its policies
  • Retention is otherwise no longer necessary

Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Third-party providers may apply their own retention periods.

15. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against:

  • Unauthorized access
  • Unauthorized disclosure
  • Alteration
  • Loss
  • Misuse

We use established service providers for authentication, cloud storage, and advertising infrastructure.

However, no internet transmission, online service, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Depending on the applicable country or region, users, parents, or legal guardians may have rights including:

  • Requesting access to personal information
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information
  • Requesting deletion
  • Objecting to certain processing
  • Restricting certain processing
  • Withdrawing consent
  • Opting out of certain advertising uses
  • Asking how information is used
  • Requesting information relating to a child under their care

Some rights vary depending on applicable law.

For information controlled directly by Google or Unity, users may also need to use the privacy-management tools provided by those companies.

17. Deleting Boing Up Data

A player, parent, or legal guardian may request deletion of game information controlled by Entexel.

Deletion may permanently remove information such as:

  • High score
  • Coins
  • Achievements
  • Unlocked cosmetic items
  • Purchased game items
  • Equipped items
  • Cloud-saved progress

After deletion, this information may not be recoverable.

Deleting Boing Up from a device does not necessarily delete cloud-stored information.

Requests for deletion of Google Account information or information controlled directly by Google must be handled through Google's account-management systems.

To request deletion of Boing Up game information, contact us using the email address provided below.

Suggested email subject: Boing Up – Data Deletion Request

Parents requesting deletion of information relating to a child may use: Boing Up – Child Data Deletion Request
