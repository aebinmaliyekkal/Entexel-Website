Entexel (“we,” “our,” or “us”) operates the mobile game Glass and Steel (“Game”). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, processed, and shared when you use Glass and Steel.

1. Information We Collect

Entexel does not operate its own analytics system and does not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information from users. However, certain information may be processed through third-party services used by the Game, including Google Play Games Services and Google AdMob.

2. Google Play Games Services

Glass and Steel may use Google Play Games Services for features such as:

  • Google account authentication or sign-in
  • Player identity
  • Achievements
  • Leaderboards and player scores
  • Game progress and supported cloud-related game features

When you sign in using Google Play Games Services, Google may provide or process information such as your Google Play Games player name, player ID or account identifier, profile information made available through Google Play Games, achievement progress, leaderboard scores, and game-related progress or saved data.

We use this information only to provide and operate the related game features. Your Google account credentials and password are not provided to Entexel.

3. Advertising

Glass and Steel uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements. For example, players may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game reward.

Google AdMob may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Device type and device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Operating system information
  • Approximate location derived from network information
  • Advertisement interactions and impressions
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Depending on your location, age, consent choices, and device settings, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Glass and Steel currently does not use separate analytics or crash-reporting services such as Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Unity Analytics. Platform services such as Google Play or Google AdMob may independently collect diagnostic, security, performance, or usage information according to their own policies.

5. In-Game Data

The Game may store information necessary for gameplay, including:

  • High scores
  • Achievements
  • Leaderboard information
  • Collected diamonds or other virtual currency
  • Unlocked balls or cosmetic items
  • Game settings
  • Game progress

Some information may be stored locally on your device, while information associated with Google Play Games features may be processed or stored through Google services.

6. In-App Purchases

Glass and Steel currently does not offer in-app purchases. Players are not required to provide payment information to Entexel in order to use the Game.

7. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain the Game
  • Authenticate players through Google services
  • Display achievements
  • Maintain leaderboards and scores
  • Save or restore supported game progress
  • Provide rewarded advertisements and advertising
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, cheating, or misuse
  • Maintain the security and functionality of the Game
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell users’ personal information.

8. Third-Party Services

Glass and Steel currently uses:

  • Google Play Games Services — for authentication, achievements, leaderboards, player identity, and supported game progress.
  • Google AdMob — for advertisements and rewarded advertisements.

These providers may collect and process information independently under their own privacy policies.

Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily mean you will stop seeing advertisements; you may instead receive non-personalized or contextual advertisements.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

  • Google Play Games Services

We retain information under our control only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Game, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, or protect the Game against fraud or abuse.

Information processed by Google is retained according to Google’s own retention policies. Local game information may remain on your device until you clear the application’s data, uninstall the Game, or the Game itself deletes or resets that information.

You may request deletion of personal information or game-related data controlled by Entexel by emailing info.entexel@gmail.com.

Please include enough information in your request for us to identify the relevant game account or data. We may need to verify your request before processing it.

Certain information may be retained where necessary for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security, or other legitimate purposes permitted by law. Information controlled directly by Google may need to be deleted or managed through your Google account or Google Play Games settings.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected in a manner that violates applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to delete the information.

Parents or legal guardians who believe a child has provided personal information through the Game may contact us at info.entexel@gmail.com.
