Your privacy matters when you play Boing Up. This page describes our game, advertising and connected-services data practices, along with how to request deletion.

At a glance

Boing Up is intended for players aged 13+. Google Play Games supports leaderboards, and Google AdMob serves advertisements. Optional Unity cloud services are described only to cover versions where they are enabled.

1. Who we are and the scope of this Policy

Boing Up (the “Game”) is published by Entexel, based in Kerala, India (“Entexel,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed, retained, and deleted when you play Boing Up on Android and use its connected services.

Different game versions and device settings may use different features. In particular, references to optional Unity Gaming Services apply only if those services are enabled in your version. Information handled independently by third-party providers is also governed by their own policies.

2. Age requirements and younger players

Boing Up is intended for players aged 13 or older. Players under 13 must not use the Game. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a player under 13 contrary to applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

Players aged 13–17 should obtain permission from a parent or legal guardian to use the Game where required. In countries that treat persons under 18 as children for data protection purposes, including India where applicable, we will follow the applicable rules on parental consent and restricted data processing. If required parental consent is not available, features involving that processing may not be offered.

We do not use this Policy to override any higher minimum age or approval requirement imposed by applicable laws or service providers.

4. How information is used

We and our service providers may process the information described above to run the Game; authenticate players; display and manage leaderboards; preserve or restore supported progress; serve, limit and measure ads; enforce gameplay integrity; prevent fraud and abuse; investigate technical issues; respond to requests; and comply with legal obligations.

Where the law requires consent for specific processing, including advertising or processing relating to minors, the relevant processing should occur only after the necessary consent has been obtained. Otherwise, processing is carried out on another legally permitted basis where available.

5. Google Play Games and leaderboards

Boing Up integrates Google Play Games Services for supported player sign-in and leaderboard functionality. Google may process your Play Games profile, player identifiers and activity under its own privacy rules. The Game may receive identifiers and profile details that Google makes available so it can associate scores with your player profile.

Your player name, score, or rank may be visible to other users through leaderboards, depending on the relevant service and your account settings. Google controls Google Account information and many Play Games privacy settings; Entexel does not receive your Google Account password.

See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising through Google AdMob

Boing Up displays advertising through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process technical and advertising data such as device information, IP address, ad impressions and interactions, and advertising identifiers when available and permitted. This helps deliver ads, measure performance, limit fraud, and support the free Game.

Ad personalization and consent options depend on your age, region, device settings and applicable law. We intend to apply age-appropriate ad restrictions for minors, including restricted or non-personalized treatment wherever required; those protections must also be correctly configured within the Game and its advertising SDK.

You may be able to manage ad privacy preferences using your Android/Google settings and any consent choice made available in the Game. See How Google uses information for ads.

7. Optional Unity services

Boing Up is built using Unity. If a published version enables Unity Gaming Services, such as Unity Authentication, Cloud Save, or Cloud Code, Unity may process a service-generated player identifier, associated game progress, settings, or technical data to provide those features. Not every Unity product mentioned here is necessarily active in every release.

For more information about processing by Unity, consult Unity's Privacy Policy. We will update this Policy if the set of active services changes in a material way.

8. Sharing and third parties

Information may be processed by or disclosed to providers that help operate connected functionality, especially Google (Play Games, AdMob, Google Play services) and Unity (only where optional services are enabled). Data may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with lawful requests, protect players, enforce our rights, investigate misuse, or facilitate a lawful business transition.

We do not sell directly collected player personal information for money. Certain advertising-related disclosures may be treated as “sharing” or targeted advertising under some laws, even if they are not a monetary sale.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep Entexel-controlled game or support information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here—for example, while an associated game profile remains active, while a support request is being resolved, or as required for security and legal compliance. Deletion requests are handled after reasonable verification; some information may be retained for a legally required or permitted period. Providers such as Google and Unity may maintain their own retention schedules for information they control.

Removing the Game or clearing its storage may delete local data but may not erase data stored by Google or by an enabled cloud service. Some progress or leaderboard records may be impossible to restore once deleted. We do not guarantee immediate removal from third-party-controlled systems.

10. Your privacy choices and requests

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may be entitled to request access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, or information about how your personal information is handled. Parents or legal guardians may also make requests on behalf of an eligible minor.

To request deletion of Boing Up data controlled by Entexel, email info.entexel@gmail.com with the subject “Boing Up – Data Deletion Request”. Include your player ID if you have it, but do not send passwords or sensitive identity documents unless specifically necessary and requested through an appropriate channel. We may ask for reasonable verification before processing the request.

Requests concerning your Google Account or data controlled independently by Google should also be managed through Google's account privacy tools. If Boing Up offers account creation within the Game, we will need to provide the additional in-app and external account-deletion options required by Google Play policy.

11. Security

We seek to use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect data under our control and rely on established platforms for connected services. Nevertheless, no data transmission, device, or storage system is guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. International processing

Google, Unity (where used), and other service providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Such processing is subject to applicable laws and provider safeguards, where required.

13. External websites and provider policies

The Game or these documents may link to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices. We encourage you to review Google's and Unity's policies if you use the corresponding features.

14. Changes to this Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when our Game, services, technology, legal requirements, or practices change. We will update the “Last updated” date above and provide additional notice when required by law. Any new version will be posted at the location where this Policy is published.
