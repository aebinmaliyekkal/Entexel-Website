A little Viking.
A mighty jump.

Boing Up is a fun, addictive endless vertical jumping game where timing, reflexes, and precision decide how high you climb.

Ride your trusty pogo stick through a world of moving platforms. Land your jumps, gather coins, unlock accessories, and chase a new personal best. The controls are easy—but the higher you go, the bigger the challenge.

More reasons to keep bouncing

Quick arcade sessions, endless challenges

Jump in for a few minutes or stay for one more run. Boing Up combines casual one-tap controls with an endless platforming challenge that rewards attention, reflexes, and precision. Every successful platform landing increases your score.

Platforms that keep you guessing

Platforms move at different speeds and can change direction at the edges of the screen. Watch where they are going before you make your next jump. Miss your landing and the rising water may catch you.

Style, progression, and treasure

Collect coins, shop for Viking helmets, caps, hats, glasses, character accessories, and pogo sticks, and explore different castle themes. Reach scoring milestones to unlock more achievements and rewards.
